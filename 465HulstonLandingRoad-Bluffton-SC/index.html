The residence

Room for everyone, steps from the pool

Built in 2019, 465 Hulston Landing Road is a two-story, 5-bedroom house on a 0.178-acre lot in Mill Creek at Cypress Ridge. The first floor is open from the dining area through the kitchen to the great room, which has an encased fireplace under the television wall. The kitchen has an oversized granite island with seating for four, white cabinetry, a gas range, stainless appliances, and a pantry, and a mudroom bench with cubbies sits by the garage entry.

The primary suite is upstairs under a tray ceiling, with a double-vanity bath, a tub, and a separate shower. The upstairs bonus space works as a second living area. Four more bedrooms share two full baths, one for each member of the family with one to spare for guests.

Out back are a screened porch, a custom paver patio with a fire pit area and seating wall, and a fenced lawn that ends at a stand of pines that won't be built on. The owner has put more than $25,000 into the house since buying it, including the aluminum fencing, new front gutters, and new landscaping.

“Plan to see the house and the Cypress Ridge amenities on the same visit. The lap pool is steps from this home.”

Talita Haggist, listing agent

Cypress Ridge

Lazy river, lap pool, and a 3-story gym

Mill Creek at Cypress Ridge sits on the SC 170 corridor in Bluffton, one of the fastest-growing municipalities in the country. The property owners association fee is $1,695.12 a year, with a $1,550 working-capital contribution at closing, and that covers the clubhouse, the 3-story fitness center, the lazy river, the lap pool, the lakes, the trails, and the courts. There is no club membership to buy.

From 465 Hulston Landing, the lap pool and its pavilion are a short walk, which is why the listing says to see the house and the amenities on the same visit.
