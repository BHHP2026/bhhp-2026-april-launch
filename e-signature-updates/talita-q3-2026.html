Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature. Click inside your BHHP signature, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V.
  4. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and click Save Changes. Gmail keeps the old signature unless you click it.
  5. Press F5 to reload Gmail, then open a new message. The report panel should read Q3 2026.

Follow Up Boss

  1. Click Copy signature above again.
  2. In Follow Up Boss, open Settings. In the Email section, under Signature, click Edit.
  3. Click inside the signature, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V.
  4. Click Save signature.

Drafts you started before today keep the old signature. Start a new message for anything you send from now on.
